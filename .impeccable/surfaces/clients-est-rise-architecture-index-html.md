# Est Rise Architecture: pitch draft (homepage)

Scope: single-page Romanian site drafted as a sales pitch to Est Rise Architecture SRL (Bucharest, founded 2023, 5.0 on Google from 3 reviews). Visitor mode: Persuade. Visitor: a Bucharest homeowner or small investor comparing architects. Action: call 0731 835 383 or send the contact form. Proof on hand: the firm's service list, its Google rating, the NodWeb 3D viewer. Constraints: project photos and 3D models are borrowed from the Apollo demo and must be labelled as demonstration images; no invented project names presented as fact; noindex.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is an A1 drawing sheet. The 3D carousel is the drawing field and the ruled title block is the navigation. This refuses the category default of a dark full-bleed photo hero with a thin grotesk name.

OWN-WORLD: Neutral plotter-paper white, ink near-black hairlines, blue for dimension lines and focus, red only for the revision stamp and primary action. Sheet frame with zone letters and numbers on the edges. Ruled title-block cells in mono; display in wide heavy Archivo. No shadows, no rounded cards, no gradients.

STORY: The visitor sees the house turning in 3D before anything else, understands that Est Rise draws and permits homes and shows them in 3D first, scans services as a drawing legend, sees the process as a revision table, and calls.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title-block header strip across the top (firm, sheet no., nav, phone). Left third: headline "Vezi casa înainte de prima cărămidă." at display scale, lede, red primary action "Programează o discuție" and the phone number. Right two thirds: the focus carousel in a framed drawing field. The centred model is live and sharp with blue dimension lines; the neighbours are blurred and recessed; a ruled cell below names the focused project.

FORM: Cartușul planșei (drawing title block), position 1 on the ordered list, taken as the pick over the rolled direction; seed key fac261b4. Signature interaction: carousel focus. The single live model-viewer moves to the centred card, its dimension lines draw in and the title-block values swap.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
