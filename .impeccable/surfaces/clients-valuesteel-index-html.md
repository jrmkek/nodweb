# ValueSteel: pitch draft (homepage)

Scope: single-page bilingual (RO default, EN toggle) site drafted as a sales pitch to VALUESTEEL SRL, a steel-structures workshop and engineering firm in Bucharest, Sector 1 (office Str. Soveja 111-113; workshop Aeroportului 4-34; 0761 984 388; office@valuesteel.ro; Google 5.0 from 3 reviews; Monday opens 08:00; valuesteel.ro claims "20+ years"). Visitor mode: Persuade. Visitor: a developer, site manager or business owner in Bucharest who needs a canopy, roof structure, carport, stair or any steel frame designed, made and installed, and wants it drawn right first. Action: call or send the contact form. Proof on hand: their own Facebook photos (space-frame domes, canopy installs with cranes, galvanised frames, welded nodes, 3D laser scans, point clouds, BIM models), Google 5.0/3, the NodWeb 3D viewer. User answers: lead with steel structures, with 3D scanning and BIM shown as how they work ("steel, drawn digitally"); carousel models are new NodWeb-authored steel demo models based on their BIM posts, labelled as demonstration. Constraints: ditch everything from the current site (style, structure, copy); no invented projects, clients, numbers or tonnages; "20+ years" and hours are the client's own statements to confirm; noindex.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The ValueSteel mark (a tapered lattice panel: two steel legs, K-bracing, orange triangles between the members) runs the page. Its lattice, redrawn as the client's hairline truss pattern, is the ground; the orange-filled triangle means "here": the focused model, the section in view, the process step, the primary action. Refuses the category default of a dark welder-with-sparks hero over an icon-card service grid (their current site).

OWN-WORLD: White ground carrying the client's truss pattern in thin brand-orange outlines; steel members drawn as black bars; brand orange #F15A29 as committed fields (the proof band, the focused-triangle fills, the primary action) with black text on it, never orange text on white. Barlow Condensed heavy uppercase display echoing the VALUESTEEL wordmark, Barlow regular body, tabular condensed labels for member IDs and counts. Square corners except the mark's own rounded panel; no shadows, no gradients.

STORY: The visitor turns a real-feeling steel structure in 3D, sees ValueSteel's own canopies and domes going up, understands the scan → model → fabricate → install path from their own photos, trusts the 5.0 rating, and calls.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar: the mark + VALUESTEEL wordmark, nav, RO/EN toggle, phone. Left: display headline in condensed caps at up to 6rem, a one-line lede, orange primary action and the phone. Right: a perspective coverflow over the lattice ground; the centred card is sharp and holds a live model-viewer with a render/3D switch; neighbours rotate away, shrink and blur. Under it a six-panel Warren-truss pager whose focused triangle fills orange, plus the structure's name, type and a member-style counter.

FORM: The ValueSteel lattice (their logo and pattern as system), a user-pinned brief ("same pattern as Starthouse, use the pattern as background or theme"), which beats the roll; seed key 602ce18b, assigned index 3 set aside for the pinned brief. Raise from the declined tensegrity-column challenger: engineering annotation discipline, node labels on leader lines with member IDs (like ST311 in their own scan screenshots) pinned to the focused model card and the process photos. Signature interaction: carousel focus. Cards spring into place, the orange fill slides along the truss pager, name and type swap.
