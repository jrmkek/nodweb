# Starthouse: pitch draft (homepage)

Scope: single-page bilingual (RO default, EN toggle) site drafted as a sales pitch to Starthouse, architecture & design office in Pecica, Arad (Str. 211 nr. 9; 0746 478 259; starthousecom@gmail.com; 94% recommend from 13 Facebook reviews; starthouse.ro does not resolve today). Visitor mode: Persuade. Visitor: a homeowner in Arad / western Romania, or someone planning a mountain cabin, comparing architects. Action: call or send the contact form. Proof on hand: the firm's own Instagram photos (cabins, glass-gable houses, flat-roof homes, work in progress), the Facebook recommendation score, the NodWeb 3D viewer. Constraints: 3D models and renders are the Apollo demo set and must be labelled as demonstration; the service list is inferred from their posts and must be confirmed with the client; no invented project names, clients or numbers; noindex. The client likes minimalism; the brief asks for "spice".

## Direction contract

THESIS: The Starthouse logo (nine isometric cubes, one dark) is the page's operating system. The dark cube means "here": it marks the focused project, the section in view and the primary action. Refuses the category default of a dark full-bleed photo hero over a white masonry gallery.

OWN-WORLD: White ground; raised things are extruded in three iso face tones (top white, left light grey, right mid grey) with ink hairline edges; ink near-black is the dark cube. Spice is one lit-window amber, reserved for the primary action, the focus marker and text selection. Schibsted Grotesk: display in heavy tight lowercase like the wordmark, body regular. No gradients, no rounded cards, no drop shadows beyond real iso extrusion.

STORY: The visitor meets their kind of house turning in 3D, sees Starthouse's real built work, reads what the office does as a set of cubes, sees the path from plot to keys, trusts the 94% recommendation, and calls.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar: iso cube mark + "starthouse®" wordmark, nav, RO/EN toggle, phone. Left column: heavy lowercase headline at display scale, one-line lede, amber primary action and the phone. Right: a perspective coverflow; the centred card is sharp, flat and holds the live model-viewer with a render/3D switch; neighbours rotate away, shrink and blur. Under it a 3x3 iso cube pager where the dark cube sits on the focused project, plus its name and type.

FORM: The Starthouse cube grid (their logo as system), position 1 on the ordered list, IMPECCABLE'S PICK chosen by the user over the rolled direction; seed key 9f19f95a. Signature interaction: carousel focus. Cards spring into their places, the dark cube hops across the pager, the name and type swap.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
