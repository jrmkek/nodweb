What happened?

Property purchases and sales

We check the title, the land register extract and any debts on the flat before you sign a pre-contract, and read the pre-contract itself. Most problems with Bucharest flats show up in the paperwork, not the viewing.

Document check before signing: from 1,200 lei, fixed.

Unpaid invoices and contract disputes

First a formal notice, which settles a surprising share of cases. If not, the fast-track payment order procedure, which usually takes months rather than years.

Formal notice: 600 lei. Court work quoted per case.

Employment

You have 45 days to challenge a dismissal decision. Bring the decision, your contract and any messages about it, and we'll tell you in the first meeting whether it's worth fighting.

Consultation: 400 lei, deducted if you instruct us.

Mihai Radu

Divorce and custody

When both of you agree, a divorce at the notary or the civil registry can be done in weeks. When you don't, we handle the court case and push for a custody arrangement the children can actually live with.

Uncontested divorce, all paperwork: from 1,800 lei.

Tax notices and inspections

Deadlines on tax decisions are short, often 45 days for an appeal. Send us a photo of the letter the day it arrives and we'll tell you what it is and what the deadline is, free.

Appeal against a tax decision: from 2,000 lei.
