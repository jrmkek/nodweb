How a full renovation runs

  1. Week 1

    Strip out

    Old floors, tiles and wiring out. Rubble leaves in bags, the same day, not piled in your stairwell.

  2. Weeks 2–3

    Wiring and pipes

    New circuits and plumbing, photographed before walls close so you have a record of where everything runs.

  3. Weeks 3–5

    Walls and screed

    Plaster, levelling, screed. The slow part, because it has to dry.

  4. Weeks 5–7

    Finishes

    Tiles, floors, paint, doors, then the kitchen and bathroom fittings.

  5. Last day

    Handover

    We walk through it with you, fix the list, clean, and give you the warranty in writing.
