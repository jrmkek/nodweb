Does a café or small shop actually need a website?

Strategy·Published 2026

Not always right away — and I'd rather tell you that honestly than sell you a site you don't need yet. Here's a genuinely useful way to think about it.

When a Google Business Profile alone is probably enough

If your customers mostly find you by searching your name or walking past, and your main needs are "show hours, show location, show a phone number," a free Google Business Profile covers a lot of that on its own. It shows up directly in Google Maps and search results, lets people leave reviews, and takes an afternoon to set up. For a lot of very local, walk-in-driven businesses, that's a reasonable starting point.

When a real website starts earning its cost

  • You take reservations or bookings. A simple form (or an embedded booking tool) removes the back-and-forth of phone calls and DMs.
  • Your menu or services change often. A page you control is faster to update than waiting on a review platform to reflect your current offering.
  • You want to look established to new customers. Before visiting somewhere new, plenty of people still check for an actual website — it reads as more permanent than a listing alone.
  • You want to rank for more than your exact name. A Business Profile helps with "your name" searches; a real site with real content is what lets you show up for broader searches like "best coffee near [area]" over time.
  • You want a stronger first impression than photos alone give. This is where something like an interactive showcase matters more — for a studio or contractor, being able to show your actual work in a properly designed space says more than a phone-camera photo dump ever will.

The honest middle ground

You don't need to choose one or the other. A Business Profile handles "can people find and contact you." A website handles "do people trust what they find, and can they act on it without picking up the phone." Most small businesses eventually want both — the question is just timing, not whether it's ever worth it.
