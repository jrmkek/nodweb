Where our pricing sits

Lower than an agency. More than a favor.

A full-service Bucharest agency typically runs €1,000–3,000 for a comparable presentation site — you're paying for their overhead as much as their output. A freelancer at the bottom of the market usually means no invoice, no contract, and no guarantee they'll answer in three months. NodWeb sits between the two on purpose: real process, real support, and a price that reflects a studio still building its client roster — not a shortcut.

FAQ

Questions worth answering up front

Do prices go up later?

As NodWeb takes on more projects, prices move up to reflect a fuller portfolio — the price you're quoted today is the price you pay, regardless of what it becomes for future clients.

What's not included in the starting price?

Domain registration and hosting (a few euros a year, on your own account so you always own it), and any stock photography or paid fonts beyond what's already built into the design system.

Can I start with the Website tier and add the 3D showcase later?

Yes — some studios start with a standard site to launch fast, then move to a custom build once they're ready to invest in the showcase experience.

Do you require the Care Plan?

No. It's there for people who'd rather not think about their site again after launch.

Know what this costs before you ask.

No quote-request wall before you can see a number. Pick your tier above, or talk through a custom project — either way, you'll know what you're getting into.
